Martingale recovery
After a loss, the next stake is multiplied to try to recover it plus a small profit on the next win. Stake resets to base after any win, or after hitting the max consecutive losses cap — whichever comes first, the bot stops trading for the day. Stake grows fast in a losing streak (e.g. $1 → $2 → $4 → $8 → $16 → $32 at 2x over 5 losses) — this does not prevent losses, it resizes them. Set the cap conservatively.
